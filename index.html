Privacy Policy for OmniCalc

Developer: Appygem (Pinki Dey)  |  Effective Date: November 2026

Summary: OmniCalc is a utility calculator, unit converter, and finance tool designed to keep your calculations private on your device. While your calculation history and notes stay strictly local, OmniCalc displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob to support free access to the app.

1. Local Data Storage

All calculation logs, formulas, unit conversion preferences, and saved entries created within OmniCalc are stored strictly on your local device storage using a local SQLite database (Room). Appygem does not collect, upload, or store your calculation data on external servers. The application does not require user registration or login.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

OmniCalc uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve banner advertisements. To deliver, personalize, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Device information, app interaction signals, and crash/diagnostic data related to ad delivery

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising anytime in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). To learn how Google uses data from partner apps, please review Google's Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

OmniCalc requests only the permissions strictly necessary to function:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to load and display advertisements via Google AdMob. All math, conversion, and finance tools continue to work offline.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide optional tactile haptic feedback on button presses.

No sensitive permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or Storage) are requested.

4. Children's Privacy

OmniCalc is a general-audience utility application and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
