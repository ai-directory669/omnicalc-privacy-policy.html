Privacy Policy for OmniCalc

Effective Date: October 2026

Summary: OmniCalc is a 100% offline utility calculator. We do not collect, track, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or user information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

OmniCalc does not collect, record, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as name, email, address, phone number, location, or device IDs). The application does not require user registration or login.

2. Local Data Storage

All calculation logs, formulas, unit conversion preferences, and notes created within OmniCalc are stored strictly on your local device storage using an encrypted-isolated SQLite database (Room). None of this data is ever sent to external cloud servers.

3. Device Permissions

OmniCalc only uses the standard VIBRATE permission to provide tactile haptic feedback when pressing keypad buttons. No sensitive permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Storage, Location, or Contacts) are requested.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

This application does not contain third-party analytics trackers, telemetry SDKs, or background web trackers.

5. Children's Privacy

Because OmniCalc does not collect any personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with child privacy laws, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.
